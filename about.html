About DrukLearn

Empowering Bhutan through digital education.

Who We Are

DrukLearn is a digital learning platform designed to make education more accessible to students across Bhutan. We bring school textbooks and learning resources together in one convenient place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make quality educational resources accessible to every student, regardless of their location. We aim to support students, teachers and lifelong learners.

Our Vision

We envision a Bhutan where every student has equal opportunities to learn, explore and achieve their educational goals through digital technology.

Our Goals

  • Provide textbooks from PP to Class XII.
  • Include Dzongkha and other school subjects.
  • Make learning resources easy to access.
  • Support students and teachers.
  • Promote digital learning throughout Bhutan.

Our Promise to Students 🇧🇹

Every student deserves the opportunity to learn. DrukLearn is built with the belief that knowledge should be accessible to everyone.

Learn Anywhere. Grow Everywhere.
